# PyAutoLens-JAX: Open-Source Strong Lensing

<img src="https://github.com/Jammy2211/PyAutoLogo/blob/main/gifs/pyautolens.gif?raw=true" width="900" />

**PyAutoLens** lets you simulate and model strong gravitational lenses, using JAX to accelerate lens modeling on GPUs. Explore examples, analyse your own observations and learn how lensing reveals the galaxies and matter along our line of sight.

## Getting Started

### Explore It Yourself

- **[Start Here](https://pyautolens.readthedocs.io/en/latest/overview/overview_1_start_here.html)** — discover PyAutoLens and work through your first example.
- **[Try it in your browser](https://colab.research.google.com/github/PyAutoLabs/autolens_workspace/blob/2026.11.9.1/start_here.ipynb)** — run the introductory notebook without installing anything.
- **[Install PyAutoLens](https://pyautolens.readthedocs.io/en/latest/installation/overview.html)** — get set up on your computer.

Visit the [workspace](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/autolens_workspace#find-your-examples) to find examples for your data and science goals.

### Explore with AI

The [**PyAutoLens AI Assistant**](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/autolens_assistant) helps you learn lensing, run examples and analyse your own data through conversation. To get started, paste this prompt into your AI coding agent:

```text
I want to do strong lensing science with PyAutoLens
(https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/PyAutoLens) and the
autolens_assistant (https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/autolens_assistant).

First clone the assistant repository, cd into it and follow its AGENTS.md.
Begin the "start here" guide for a new user.
```

See [AI Options](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/PyAutoScientist/blob/main/AI_OPTIONS.md) for tools and setup guidance.

## Learn Lensing

[**HowToLens**](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/HowToLens) teaches gravitational lensing and Bayesian inference through worked notebooks, starting from undergraduate level.

The [PyAutoLens documentation](https://pyautolens.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html) covers the software’s features and how to use them in your own analysis.
